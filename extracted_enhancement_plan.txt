4. Enhancements ranked by gain per hour
Deadline 10 Nov 23:59 IST, about 9 hours left. Tier 1 is mandatory; Tier 2 only if Tier 1 is done by ~18:00.
Tier 1 (≈ 2 h)
☐ Fix reproducibility (section 1). This is the one that can cost the prize outright.
☐ Exclude festival windows from training and climatology: Diwali 2025 (18–22 Oct 2025) and 7–10 Nov 2026 evenings/nights, or add an is_festival_window feature so the trees isolate them. Drop the 3× weight on Oct/Nov, or apply it only to non-festival hours.
☐ Replace anchor_latest with a robust, festival-free anchor: the 1–7 Nov station × hour profile, optionally scaled by a recent-level ratio capped at ~1.3.
☐ Add the 30-line rolling-origin backtest and fit the climatology/recent blend weight and α on it instead of guessing.
☐ Drop dayofweek as a tree feature; keep is_weekend.
Tier 2 (≈ 2–3 h)
☐ Make LightGBM a real multi-horizon model: training rows = (origin, station, horizon) with anchor stats computed at origin, horizon_h, days_since_diwali; target = value at origin + h. The backtest says recent-level features win.
☐ Calibrate Model D on history (Open-Meteo archive for 2025–26, then regress), or drop it and give its weight to the profile member.
Tier 3 (only if everything else is in)
☐ Open-Meteo Air Quality (CAMS) PM2.5 forecast as a bias-corrected member.
5. Making the two submissions bracket the uncertainty
