About the data, the models and what runs without keys.

  • Google MapsStation map

Does it need API keys?

No. Without keys you get the built-in map, estimated Google readings and a rule-based advisory, each labelled.

Where is the PM2.5 data from?

IITM and BMC monitors, via the Zindi challenge dataset. Weather is from Open-Meteo.

How fresh are the forecasts?

Readings stop at 10 Nov 2026, 09:00 IST. Forecasts run to 21 Nov.

What are Submission 1 and 2?

Our two entries: Submission 1 blends CatBoost and LightGBM equally, Submission 2 is CatBoost alone.

Is the advisory written by AI?

With a Gemini key, yes, and the model is named. Otherwise fixed rules write it.
